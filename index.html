About Me

I believe that every journey starts with a small step.

I started my content creation journey without knowing where it would take me. In the beginning, I never imagined that I would come this far or that brands would one day reach out to me for collaborations and PR packages.

I simply started creating content because I enjoyed it.

People sometimes teased me for posting many pictures and videos. Some people did not understand why I was putting so much effort into social media.

But I decided to focus on myself instead of focusing on what others were saying. I kept learning, creating, improving, and showing up.

Today, when I receive a PR package or get an opportunity to work with a brand, I feel genuinely happy. It reminds me of how far I have come from where I started. My journey is still going on, and I am excited about what comes next.

I never thought that the small things I started doing for fun would become such an important part of my life.

My Content Creation Journey

01

The Beginning

I started my journey with simple posts, photos, and videos. At that time, I was only doing it because I enjoyed creating and sharing.

02

The Learning Phase

I slowly started learning about content creation, photography, video making, editing, Instagram, and how to present products and experiences in a better way.

03

The Consistency Phase

I understood that growth does not happen overnight. So, I focused on being consistent and continued creating even when the results were not immediate.

04

The Confidence Phase

With time, I became more confident in front of the camera and started exploring different types of content. I also started believing more in my own creativity.

05

The Collaboration Phase

Eventually, I started receiving opportunities from brands and businesses. Getting my first PR packages and collaboration opportunities was a very special feeling for me.

06

Where I Am Today

Today, I am happy to call myself a content creator and influencer. I continue to work with brands, create promotional content, share products and experiences, and build my own identity through content.

07

What’s Next

This is not the end of my journey. I want to keep learning, growing, creating better content, and reaching more people through my work.
